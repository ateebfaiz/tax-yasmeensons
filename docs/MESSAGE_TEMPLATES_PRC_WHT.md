# Official Tax Certificate Request Templates (English & Urdu)
**Yasmeen & Sons Tax Practice — Client Communication Folio**

Ready-to-use message and email templates for requesting vital Withholding Tax (WHT) certificates, remittance proofs, and deduction statements from banks, telecom providers, and utilities.

---

## 1. Freelancers: Proceeds Realization Certificate (PRC) / Encashment
**Audience**: Freelancers, IT Exporters, Remote Workers, Consultants receiving foreign funds.  
**Purpose**: Legal proof of foreign inward remittance to claim export tax exemptions or 0.25%–1% concessional final tax under Section 154A.

### Email / Letter (English)
```text
Subject: Request for Proceeds Realization Certificate (PRC) / Remittance Encashment Certificate

Dear Customer Support Team,

I am writing to formally request the Proceeds Realization Certificate (PRC) / Remittance Encashment Certificate for the foreign funds credited to my account.

Account Details:
- Account Title: [Your Full Name]
- Account Number / IBAN: [Your Account Number]
- CNIC Number: [Your CNIC]

Transaction Details:
- Approximate Date of Credit: [Date or Month]
- Amount Received: [Amount and Currency, e.g., $500 USD]
- Remitter Name/Source: [e.g., Upwork, Fiverr, Payoneer, Client Name]

These documents are urgently required for filing my income tax returns and updating my wealth statement with the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR). Please issue the certificate digitally or notify me when it is ready for collection at my home branch.

Thank you for your prompt assistance.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Contact Number]
```

### اردو رہنمائی (Urdu Guidance)
> یہ ای میل اپنے بینک کی ہیلپ لائن یا برانچ مینیجر کو بھیجیں۔ اس سرٹیفکیٹ کی بنیاد پر فری لانسنگ اور آئی ٹی برآمدات کی آمدنی پر قانونی ٹیکس چھوٹ (Section 154A) حاصل کی جاتی ہے اور دولت کے گوشوارے میں جائز بیرونی ترسیلات کے طور پر درج کی جاتی ہے۔

## 2. Bank Withholding Tax Certificate (Section 164)
**Audience**: Salaried, Pensioners, Housewives, Business Filers.  
**Purpose**: Claim tax credits against income tax for cash withdrawal taxes, profit on debt withholding, or international debit/credit card advance tax (Section 236Y).

### Email / Helpline Request (English)
```text
Subject: Request for Annual Withholding Tax Certificate (Section 164) – Tax Year 2026

Dear Sir/Madam,

Please issue the Annual Withholding Tax (WHT) Certificate under Section 164 of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001, for my account for the period covering July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2026.

Account Details:
- Account Title: [Your Full Name]
- Account Number: [Your Account Number]
- CNIC Number: [Your CNIC]

Kindly email a digital PDF copy of the tax certificate to this email address or provide a downloadable link through the internet banking portal.

Thank you.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[Your Contact Number]
```

### اردو رہنمائی (Urdu Guidance)
> سیکشن 164 سرٹیفکیٹ سے بینک میں کٹا ہوا تمام ایڈوانس ودہولڈنگ ٹیکس (منافع پر ٹیکس، کیش نکالنے یا کارڈ ٹرانزیکشنز پر ٹیکس) آپ کے سالانہ ٹیکس ریٹرن میں ایڈجسٹ ہو کر ٹیکس واجب الادا رقم کو کم کر دیتا ہے۔

## 3. Mobile SIM Annual Tax Certificate (WhatsApp / Helpline)
**Audience**: All Individuals (Every active cellular subscriber).  
**Purpose**: Claim Section 236 advance tax charged on every mobile scratch card, easyload, and monthly postpaid/prepaid bundle.

### WhatsApp / SMS Request (English)
```text
Hello Team,

I need the Annual Tax Certificate / Withholding Tax Statement for my mobile number for the tax year ending June 30, 2026.

SIM Details:
- Mobile Number: [Your Mobile Number]
- Network: [Jazz / Zong / Telenor / Ufone]
- SIM Owner Name: [Your Full Name]
- CNIC Number: [Your CNIC]

Please share the digital tax certificate via PDF in this chat or email it to [Your Email].

Thank you.
```

### Direct Operator Shortcodes & Self-Service Portals
- **Jazz**: Send `WHT` or `TAX` to `6767`, or download via Jazz World App.
- **Telenor**: Type `TAX` and SMS to `345`, or via My Telenor App.
- **Zong**: Send `Tax` to `310` or download certificate via My Zong App.
- **Ufone**: Dial `*336*1#` or generate via Ufone App (Tax Certificate menu).

## 4. Electricity Bill Advance Tax Certificate (Section 235)
**Audience**: Domestic, Commercial, and Industrial power consumers.  
**Purpose**: Claim advance tax levied on domestic electricity bills exceeding PKR 25,000/month or commercial connections under Section 235.

### Request Letter / DISCO Helpline (English)
```text
Subject: Request for Annual Income Tax Deduction Certificate on Electricity Bill (Section 235)

Dear Billing Officer / Customer Support Team,

I am requesting the Annual Income Tax Deduction Certificate under Section 235 of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001, for electricity connection registered at my premises for Tax Year 2026.

Connection Details:
- Consumer ID / Reference Number: [14-Digit Reference Number on Bill]
- Registered Consumer Name: [Name printed on Electricity Bill]
- Installation Address: [Premises Address]
- Applicant Name & CNIC: [Your Name and CNIC]

Please provide the deduction summary showing total billing and total income tax deducted during July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2026.

Regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Phone Number]
```

### اردو رہنمائی (Urdu Guidance)
> اگر بجلی کے بل پر انکم ٹیکس (Advance Tax) کٹ رہا ہے تو اس کا ریفرنس نمبر اور سالانہ ادائیگی ٹیکس گوشوارے میں درج کر کے رقم کلیم کی جا سکتی ہے۔

```text
Dear Accounts & Finance Office,

Please issue the Annual Advance Tax Deduction Certificate under Section 236I of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001, for the tuition fees paid for student [Student Name], Roll / Registration Number [Student ID], for Tax Year 2026.

Student & Guardian Details:
- Student Name: [Student Name]
- Student CNIC / B-Form: [CNIC/B-Form]
- Guardian / Sponsor Name: [Parent/Guardian Full Name]
- Guardian CNIC: [Parent/Guardian CNIC]

Thank you.

Sincerely,
[Guardian Name]
[Contact Number]
```
