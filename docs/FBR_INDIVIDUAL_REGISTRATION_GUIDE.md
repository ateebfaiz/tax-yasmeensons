**Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) — Iris Portal**

## 1. Overview
To register as an individual taxpayer with the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) and obtain a National Tax Number (NTN), an applicant registers through the official FBR Iris Portal (`https://iris.fbr.gov.pk`). 

Once registered, the individual is assigned an NTN (identical to their 13-digit CNIC) and can file their annual income tax returns and wealth statements under the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001.

> **Zero-Credential Policy**: Yasmeen & Sons **never** requests or stores your FBR Iris password or 4-digit PIN. We assist in preparing figures, gathering documentation, generating reconciliations, and guiding you through official Iris submissions.

## 2. Required Information & Documents

| # | Item | Detailed Specification | Requirements & Exceptions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Valid CNIC** | 13-digit NADRA Computerized National Identity Card | Must be valid. Entered without dashes on Iris (`35202XXXXXXXXX`). |
| 2 | **Active Mobile SIM** | Registered with cellular operator (Jazz/Zong/Telenor/Ufone) | Must be registered under applicant's **own CNIC** to receive the SMS OTP. **Exception**: If not in applicant's name, must be on an **immediate blood relative** (Father/Mother/Spouse/Sibling) with relative's full name and CNIC recorded. |
| 3 | **Personal Email Address** | Active inbox to receive official FBR notifications & email OTP | If applicant does not have an email account, **our team provides full guidance to create a secure personal email address**. |
| 4 | **Residential Address** | Full physical address including house/flat #, street, area, city, and district | Specify property ownership status: Owner, Tenant, Shared Parental, or Official Accomodation. |
| 5 | **Paid Utility Bill** | Latest paid bill (Electricity, Gas, or Landline) within the last 3 months | Must clearly show the **Consumer Number / Reference Number** and address matching or near the residence. |
| 6 | **Income Proof** | Employer salary certificate, pension book, or remittance certificates | For salaried: 12 months payslips or Section 149 certificate. For freelancers: PRC/Encashment certificates. |

## 3. Overseas Pakistanis & Non-Residents (NICOP / POC / Passport)

Overseas Pakistanis who do not possess a Pakistani cellular SIM registered under their name follow special Iris provisions:
- **Identifier**: National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis (NICOP) or Pakistan Origin Card (POC).
- **Mobile Number**: Can enter a foreign mobile number or blood relative's local number.
- **Verification**: FBR Iris sends dual OTP codes to the **registered email address** for overseas applicants instead of requiring a local biometric cellular network PIN.

## 4. Step-by-Step Registration Procedure on FBR Iris

### Step 1: Access the Iris Registration Portal
1. Visit `https://iris.fbr.gov.pk`.
2. Click **Registration for Unregistered Person** on the login screen.

### Step 2: Enter Basic Particulars
- **CNIC / NTN**: Enter 13 digits without hyphens.
- **Prefix**: Select `Mr.`, `Ms.`, or `Mrs.`
- **First Name & Last Name**: Exactly as printed on the CNIC.
- **Service Provider & Cell No**: Select telecom network and enter `00923XXXXXXXXX`.
- **Email Address**: Enter applicant's primary email.

### Step 3: Address & Property Particulars
- **Property Type**: Residential / Commercial.
- **Form**: House / Flat / Plot / Apartment.
- **Measurement**: Area in Marla / Kanal / Sq. Ft.
- **Unit / Street / City**: Specific address details.
- **Capacity**: Owner / Tenant / Benami / Occupant.

### Step 4: OTP Verification & Password Issuance
1. Click **Submit**. FBR dispatches two distinct 6-digit codes:
   - One code to the mobile SIM via SMS.
   - One code to the email inbox.
2. Enter both codes into the verification screen.
3. Upon successful verification, FBR automatically generates:
   - **User ID**: 13-digit CNIC.
   - **Password**: Sent via SMS and email.
   - **4-Digit PIN**: Used for final submission of all tax returns and wealth reconciliations.

## 5. Post-Registration: Active Taxpayer List (ATL) Status

- Obtaining an NTN creates an FBR registration but does **not** automatically grant "Filer" / "Active" status on the Active Taxpayer List.
- To become an **Active Filer** and benefit from 50% reduced withholding tax rates on banking, property, and vehicle transactions:
  1. Complete and submit the annual Income Tax Return (`Form 114(1)`) and Wealth Statement (`Form 116`).
  2. If filing after the statutory deadline, pay the ATL Surcharge (`Section 182A`) via FBR e-Payment PSID (PKR 1,000 for individuals).
